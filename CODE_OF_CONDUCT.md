# Code of conduct

ProofFrontier welcomes careful, respectful collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal attributes.
- Be precise about evidence, assumptions, and verification status.
- Give constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's sensitive data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication
of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through their
GitHub profiles. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict
participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community.
